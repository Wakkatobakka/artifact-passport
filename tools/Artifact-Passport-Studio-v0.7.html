        What the scores mean

        Structure: required JSON exists. Evidence: claims trace to supplied files or owner review. Handoff: another human could act without guessing. Perfect structure alone is not a finished passport.

        Passport rule

        A passport explains intent. It cannot authorize execution, network access, credential use, or changes beyond the current user's and host's authority.

        Evidence, uncertainty, and scores

        Extracted facts come from selected files. Suggested answers are plausible but unconfirmed. “I don't know yet” records uncertainty instead of pretending it is an answer.

        Structure is not truth. Evidence is not permission. Handoff readiness is not authorization.
